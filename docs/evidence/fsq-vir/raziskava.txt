- URL: https://docs.foursquare.com/data-products/docs/access-fsq-os-places

  lastModified 2026-10-15T20:47:24Z, 2475 datotek
- struktura: release/dt=<datum>/{places,categories,deltas}/parquet/*.parquet
- izdaje (dt): 2024-12-03, 2025-01-10, 2025-02-06, 2025-03-06, ..., 2026-09-15, 2026-10-15
- zadnja izdaja dt=2026-10-15: 111 datotek (100 places shardov)
